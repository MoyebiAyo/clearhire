THE PROBLEM

Recruiters do not have a screening problem. They have a consistency problem.

Volume turns structured decisions into first-impression contests. Scheduling and closure break downstream.

300

applications

More candidates than one person can properly read.

40

CVs read

Fatigue changes the quality of the decision.

0

closure

Rejected candidates often receive silence.

THE INSIGHT

Do not automate the hiring decision. Automate the noise around it.

Blind first. Explain the evidence. Reveal later. Then schedule, remind, and close.

Only job-relevant evidence enters scoring.

02
